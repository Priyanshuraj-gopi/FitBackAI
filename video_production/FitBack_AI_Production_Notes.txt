5. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE EXPLAINED
- RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):
    • Vector embedding & semantic search over technical spec sheets (KRT-101), fabric elasticity indexes, and brand return SOPs.
    • Eliminates model hallucinations by grounding advice in real flat pattern cm measurements.
- MCP (Model Context Protocol):
    • Standardized JSON-RPC 2.0 tool orchestration layer connecting the FitBack-AI agent with 6 core enterprise backends:
      1. CRM (Customer Profile & consent)
      2. OMS (Order Management)
      3. Inventory (Warehouse stock checks)
      4. Logistics (BlueDart reverse pickup booking)
      5. Telemetry (Granular fit complaint logging)
      6. Sizing Engine (Human-approved size chart drafting)

6. RESPONSIBLE AI & ETHICAL COMMITMENTS
- Zero Body Shaming: Explains fit deltas strictly in terms of garment pattern ease, never customer body weight or shape.
- Privacy & Consent: Optional measurements, explicit consent toggles for data storage and purchase history indexing.
- Human-in-the-Loop Governance: Machine learning detects 70% bust tightness patterns and proposes +2cm corrections; NO size chart is ever committed to production without explicit human authorization.
- Financial Accuracy: Illustrative calculations strictly separate gross order value retained (revenue) from net profit.
