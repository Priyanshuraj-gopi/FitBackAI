[ACT 1 — THE PAIN]
Timeline: 00:00 – 00:45 (Duration: 45s)
Tone: Empathetic, Thoughtful, Relatable
Visuals:
  - 00:00 - 00:12: Cinematic slow-motion tracking shot of an Indian ethnic-wear studio. Warm ivory backdrop, intricate cotton embroidery.
  - 00:12 - 00:24: Customer Priya browsing an ethnic-wear store on her mobile, hesitating between Size S, M, and L. She selects Size M.
  - 00:24 - 00:35: Unboxing the courier parcel. Priya tries on the kurta; notice tension at shoulders and chest pulling awkwardly.
  - 00:35 - 00:45: Disappointed expression. Customer initiates return request on phone. Courier pickup at door. Returned parcel entering a sorting warehouse.
On-Screen Title:
  "ONE WRONG SIZE. MULTIPLE BUSINESS COSTS."
  Transition: "WHAT IF CUSTOMER SERVICE COULD PREDICT, ACT, AND LEARN?"

Narration (Voice: Natural Indian English, Warm & Professional):
"Online fashion shopping has made beautiful ethnic wear accessible to everyone. But one question still creates uncertainty: will the outfit actually fit?
Priya finds a beautiful kurta, selects size M, and places her order.
But when it arrives, the fit is uncomfortable. The shoulders and bust are too tight.
She requests a return.
For Priya, this is a disappointing experience. For the business, it means additional shipping costs, reverse logistics, inventory handling, and potentially selling the returned product again at a discount.
Traditional customer service reacts after the problem happens.
But what if we could prevent the wrong fit before the customer even places the order?"

[ACT 2 — THE AGENT ARRIVES]
Timeline: 00:45 – 01:45 (Duration: 60s)
Tone: Optimistic, Dynamic, Confident
Visuals:
  - 00:45 - 00:55: Cinematic reveal of FitBack-AI brand logo. Clean typography (Playfair Display) with terracotta & warm ivory palette.
  - 00:55 - 01:15: PREVENT: Priya exploring Ivory Floral Cotton Kurta (KRT-101). FitBack-AI chat assistant opens: "How do you prefer your kurtas to fit?" Numerical comparison against flat table specs (Size M: 92cm bust vs Size L: 98cm bust). Recommendation card: Size L recommended (+8cm ease) with 94% confidence.
  - 01:15 - 01:32: RECOVER: Guided 5-Step Exchange Assistant. Instant order verification (#FW-2026-1042), live warehouse inventory check (8 units of Size L in stock), 1-click doorstep exchange scheduled via BlueDart.
  - 01:32 - 01:45: LEARN: Admin intelligence dashboard showing live feedback ingestion. Insight alert: "Upper-body tightness pattern detected on KRT-101."
On-Screen Badge:
  "PREVENT. RECOVER. LEARN."

Narration:
"Meet FitBack-AI, an intelligent customer-service agent designed to prevent, recover, and learn from fit-related returns.
Before purchase, FitBack-AI understands the customer's fit preferences, retrieves the product's size chart, and compares available measurements.
It provides a recommendation with an explanation, helping customers make more informed decisions.
The customer remains free to choose another size.
If a fit problem still occurs, FitBack-AI helps recover the customer experience.
It checks the order, verifies the applicable policy, checks inventory, and offers an eligible exchange.
With the customer's permission, it can initiate the appropriate workflow.
The objective is to retain the sale wherever possible while respecting the customer's choice.
Finally, FitBack-AI learns from every return.
It identifies recurring product-level fit problems and prepares useful insights for the brand's quality team."

  "WORKING PROTOTYPE + SIMULATED INTEGRATIONS"

Narration:
"FitBack-AI uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation, also known as RAG.
RAG allows the agent to search the brand's own documents before generating an answer.
These documents include product catalogues, size charts, fabric descriptions, customer feedback, and return policies.
When Priya asks which size she should choose, the agent retrieves the relevant information and uses it to provide a more grounded recommendation.
This reduces reliance on unsupported guesses.
RAG helps FitBack-AI retrieve the right knowledge. MCP helps it connect with the business systems needed to take action.
Through standardised tool connections, the agent can check orders, retrieve inventory information, verify exchange policies, and initiate permitted workflows.
This is what makes it an AI agent rather than simply a chatbot.
Some integrations in this prototype may be simulated using sample data. These must be clearly labelled as simulated rather than presented as live production connections."

[ACT 4 — SOMETHING GOES WRONG]
Timeline: 02:45 – 03:30 (Duration: 45s)
Tone: Serious, Thoughtful, Principled
Visuals:
  - 02:45 - 03:00: Return Analytics breakdown of 100 historical returns for KRT-101.
    40 Shoulders tight, 30 Bust tight (70% upper body constriction).
  - 03:00 - 03:15: System anomaly alert: AI generates automated size-chart correction proposal.
    Error Highlight: The automated agent confuses flat garment measurements with body circumference and fit ease allowances.
    Banner: "DRAFT — HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED".
  - 03:15 - 03:30: Human Quality Manager Sunil Mehta audits the proposal on the dashboard. He identifies the false pattern assumption and clicks "REJECT".
    Audit Log stamp: "CHANGE REJECTED — NOT PUBLISHED. Production size chart protected."
On-Screen Callout:
  "AI ASSISTS. HUMANS REMAIN IN CONTROL."

Narration:
"But AI systems are not perfect.
While analysing sample return records, FitBack-AI identifies a pattern of upper-body fit complaints and proposes a size-chart correction.
However, the agent makes an incorrect assumption about the measurements.
A human quality manager reviews the underlying information, catches the issue, and rejects the proposed change.
FitBack-AI never automatically publishes production size-chart changes.
It supports human decisions. It does not replace human accountability."
